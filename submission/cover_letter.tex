Dear Editors,

We herewith submit our manuscript entitled \emph{Start Smart: Better Initialization for Numerical Estimation of Multinomial Probit Models} for consideration for publication in Transportation Research Part B: Methodological.

Discrete choice models are widely applied in different
contexts within transportation science, with the
foremost application probably being constituted by mode
choice models. The numerical estimation of such models
is not trivial. In particular the application of probit models has suffered from numerical complexities, such as long run times and failure to converge to the global optimum.

One issue in this respect is the choice of the initial
estimate for numerical search procedures. This paper
contributes to the literature by proposing a new
algorithm for providing an initial guess. The proposal
is shown to lead to consistent estimates (under
appropriate assumptions) while being numerically cheap
mainly using regressions and kernels to covariance
matrices.
While the approach is geared towards probit models, it is also more broadly applicable.

Besides the theoretical justification, the paper contains
an extensive simulation exercise and a real-world case study,
both showing that the method is effective.
Therefore, we are confident that the proposed method is a significant addition to the toolbox of transportation scientists.

The procedures presented are implemented in the R package Rprobit.
Replication code and results from the paper are openly available at
\url{https://github.com/loelschlaeger/startsmart}. The manuscript is based on
Chapter~4 of the first author's doctoral thesis
(Bielefeld University, 2026, \url{https://pub.uni-bielefeld.de/record/3014719})
and is otherwise neither published nor under consideration elsewhere. Both
authors have approved the manuscript and declare no competing interests.
